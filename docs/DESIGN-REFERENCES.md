# Design references

What we borrow from leading non-profit sites, and where it shows up on AWF. Revisit this file
before designing any new page.

## Principles

1. **Credibility first.** Sponsors and diplomats look for evidence: a one-line mission, real photos,
   impact numbers, governance, partners. Put these above the fold and repeat them often.
2. **Show where the money goes.** Every ask is tied to something concrete.
3. **One clear path to act.** "Give" or "Partner" is never more than two clicks away.
4. **Dignity.** Show people as capable participants, not objects of pity. No identifiable children
   without consent; tell stories at the level of a program.
5. **Calm, editorial look.** Generous whitespace, a serif for display headings, restrained colour,
   subtle motion only.

## Visual direction (finalized in Phase 2)

- **Colours, from the logo:** deep navy (primary), sky blue (accent), saffron/gold (calls to action),
  warm off-white backgrounds.
- **Type:** a serif for display headings (Fraunces or Source Serif 4) and Inter for body text.
  Sentence-case headings, not all-caps.
- **Layout:** full-bleed photo hero, impact-stats band, alternating story sections, a strong footer
  with transparency links.
